01

Pulse

Real-time streaming lakehouse for NSE/BSE market data

Market ticks stream through Kafka into a Delta Lake medallion on object storage. Spark Structured Streaming lands raw ticks in bronze, cleans them into silver and rolls them up into one-minute OHLC candles in gold. Trino serves the tables to dbt marts and live dashboards, Great Expectations gates data quality, and Airflow runs the batch side: quality, dbt and Delta compaction.

  • 10,000+ticks per second from the producer
  • 3 layersbronze, silver, gold in Delta Lake
  • 1 commanddocker compose up starts all of it

03

Sentinel-RAG

Question answering over SEBI and RBI regulation, with citations

Ask a question about Indian securities and banking regulation and get an answer that cites the exact passage it came from. Hybrid retrieval (BM25 plus dense vectors, fused with reciprocal rank fusion), a cross-encoder reranker on ONNX Runtime, and an agent loop that rewrites and retries when retrieval is weak, refuses when nothing relevant is found, and falls back to quoting passages if the LLM is unavailable. Guardrails, OpenTelemetry tracing and a hardened public API included.

  • 0.90Hit@1 with hybrid retrieval and reranking (BM25 alone: 0.58)
  • 1.00Hit@5 on 50 answerable golden questions
  • 62 / 62answer-or-refuse decisions correct at the calibrated threshold

06

Schedule my medicines

Hackathon concept: a daily medicine timetable inside the NHS App

Built with Team Double Helix at the Leeds AI Society’s AI in Healthcare Hackathon, October 2026. People on ten or more medicines were asking chatbots to plan their day. Our prototype does it safely: it combines a patient’s prescriptions with their routine and rules drawn from NHS guidance, then an explainable scheduler and an independent checker produce the timetable. Language models help with forms and explanations only, never the schedule itself. Synthetic patient data throughout.

  • Rules, not a modeldecide every dose time
  • 3 languagesEnglish, Polish and Urdu
  • 2 daysfrom idea to a deployed prototype on AWS
